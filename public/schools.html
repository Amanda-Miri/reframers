Reframers is a hands-on creative thinking workshop for children. Kids make their own glasses, explore everyday situations through stories and games, and practise asking, “What else could be true?”

A creative group experience

For curious
young minds

Sessions are designed for groups who want a creative, age-appropriate way to explore perspective-taking. Children create a pair of handmade Reframers and use them in guided activities.

  • Designed for children aged 7–10
  • Creative making, games and story-based examples
  • For schools, libraries, councils and community groups
  • Format and availability discussed with each organiser
