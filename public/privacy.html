A draft for review before publication

Privacy Policy

This draft describes the intended handling of information on the Reframers website. It must be checked against the final email platform, hosting and business contact details before publication.

Draft prepared: 20 October 2026

Who we are

Reframers is a children's creative thinking workshop founded by Amanda Doggett in Melbourne, Australia. In this draft, “we” means the Reframers website operator. Before publication, add the correct legal business name, ABN if applicable, postal address and privacy contact email.

Information we may collect

If you submit a form, we may collect your first name, email address, suburb, a child's age range, how you heard about Reframers, or details about a school or community enquiry. We do not ask for a child's name, photograph, school, health details or date of birth through the initial interest form.

Why we use it

We use information to respond to enquiries, contact parents about workshop updates they requested, manage the children's book waitlist, provide requested resources, and improve the website. Optional marketing updates are sent only where you have agreed to receive them.

Email platform and service providers

The planned email platform is MailerLite, subject to account access and configuration. Before launch, confirm what data is sent to MailerLite, the relevant audience groups, storage locations, retention settings and any overseas disclosures. Hosting, spam prevention and analytics providers must also be identified here before publication.

Children's information

The website is directed to parents and adult organisers. Forms should be completed by an adult. Child age is requested only to check whether the workshop is relevant and should be retained only as long as needed for that purpose. We do not intentionally collect children's names or contact details through these forms.

Consent and unsubscribing

Workshop updates, book updates and school enquiry follow-up are separate purposes. Each form should explain the relevant purpose. Marketing emails should include a clear way to unsubscribe. Unsubscribing from one list should not automatically enrol or remove you from another list unless required to honour your request or applicable law.

Access, correction and complaints

You can request access to or correction of personal information, ask a question about privacy, or make a complaint using the privacy contact details that will be added before launch. We will respond in accordance with applicable Australian privacy requirements.

Security and retention

We plan to use secure form handling, restricted access, spam controls and data minimisation. Information should be deleted or de-identified when it is no longer needed, subject to legal obligations. The final policy should describe actual retention periods and security processes.

Cookies, analytics and links

The preview does not currently use a configured analytics provider. If analytics or cookies are added, this policy must identify them and explain the choices available. External websites have their own privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or its service providers change. The effective date will be shown at the top of the published version.

Publication checklist: confirm legal entity and contact details, MailerLite configuration, analytics provider, spam protection, retention periods and contact process before replacing this draft notice.
